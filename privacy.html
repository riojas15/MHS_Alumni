# Privacy Policy **Laredo Martin High School Alumni Directory** **Last Updated: October 26, 2026** ## 1. Our Commitment to Your Privacy The Laredo Martin High School Alumni Directory respects your privacy. Our purpose is to help former classmates reconnect and stay in touch. We are committed to protecting your personal information. **We will never sell, rent, or trade your personal information to anyone.** ## 2. Information We Collect When you register for the directory, we may collect the following information: - First and last name - Graduation year - Mailing address - Telephone number - Email address - Account login information We collect this information to help alumni identify and communicate with former classmates. ## 3. Who Can See Your Information? Our directory is intended exclusively for registered members whose accounts have been approved. We do not intend for personal directory information to be publicly available to visitors who are not logged in and approved. Members may choose which information they want to share with other members, where those privacy options are available. Please remember that information you choose to share with other members may be seen, copied, or saved by those members. ## 4. How We Use Your Information We use your information only to: - Maintain the alumni directory. - Help former classmates reconnect. - Allow members to communicate with one another. - Manage member accounts and approve registrations. - Maintain the security and proper operation of the website. We do not sell personal information or use it for commercial advertising. ## 5. Email and Private Messages Your email address and other contact information will not be intentionally disclosed to other members when you use the website's private messaging features, except as necessary to provide a feature you have chosen to use. We may send emails related to account registration, email confirmation, password resets, and website administration. Email delivery services may process information as necessary to deliver these messages. ## 6. Protecting Your Information We use the security and privacy features available through our website hosting and database services to help protect your information from unauthorized access. However, no website or electronic storage system can be guaranteed to be completely secure. Members are responsible for keeping their login credentials confidential. ## 7. Third-Party Services Our website uses third-party services, including GitHub Pages for website hosting and Supabase for account authentication and database services. These providers may process certain information as necessary to operate their services. Their handling of information is also subject to their respective privacy policies and terms. ## 8. Your Choices You may choose which personal information to share with other members using the available privacy settings. If you would like to correct or remove your information, or close your account, please contact the website administrator. We will make reasonable efforts to address your request. ## 9. Changes to This Policy This Privacy Policy may be updated as the website develops. Any changes will be posted on this page, along with the revised update date. ## 10. Contact Us If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or how your information is handled, please contact the Laredo Martin High School Alumni Directory administrator through the contact method provided on the website. --- **Our Promise:** This directory exists to help former classmates reconnect. Your personal information is not for sale, and we are committed to respecting your privacy.
